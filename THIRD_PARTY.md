# Third-Party Assets

## Pixora Icons

The editor icons in `src/ui/pixora/` are derived from **Pixora Icons** by
[tsora1603](https://github.com/tsora1603), licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

- Source: https://github.com/tsora1603/pixora-icons
- Files: `zed.png`, `vscode.png`, `gnome-text-editor.png`, `nvim.png`,
  `vim.png`, `micro.png`
- Changes: the upstream SVG artwork was rasterized to 16x16 RGBA PNGs; zinc
  nearest-neighbor scales them to 32x32 at runtime. The icons are used only to
  identify detected editors in the settings UI.

Upstream also credits **Tux on Pixels** by maxtron95 (CC0 1.0) as a source.
